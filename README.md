## Enunciado 
Desafios de projecto C
- Purpose: design a persuasive Anthropocene Simulator
- Context: Consider the challenge of a very hard to play game of
- world simulation of the ever more predictable climate change;
- Relevance:
    - a sim that is speculative and perception of consequences
    - model across geographies, low detail but global (research)
    - a sim that combines local action with global emergent outcomes
    - explore a soundscape for emotional effect
    - go for the inescapable Venus scenario
